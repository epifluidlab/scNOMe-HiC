## Deliberately not included

- **`scnomehic_paper/s3/plot_dualaxis.py`** — a script twin of this notebook,
  same method and same outputs. The notebook is the shipped form; keeping both
  would be two copies of one figure's code.
- **`s3/analysis/{ctcf,tss_cgi}/`** — the matrices, region BEDs and deepTools
  figures. Generated by `05_profiles/`.
- **`s3/make_readable_profiles.py`** — reformats `.profile.tsv` into a
  human-readable table. A convenience for inspecting numbers, not part of any
  panel.
- The `s3.ipynb` distribution work and the `s3/snakemake/` barnyard project also
  live in that folder — those are **Supplementary Figure S4**, migrated to
  `../figS4/`.

## Non-CGI TSS

The notebook also draws `GCH_HCG.TSS_nonCGI.dualaxis.pdf`, which is not an S3
panel. It is kept because it is what shows the non-CGI signal is real rather than
absent — see the README's table.
